The kitchen clock showed an impossible hour, and nobody wanted to ask why.

"I left the key under the *blue* flowerpot," I said without looking back.

The window was open, although the storm seemed to be coming from inside the house.

"Don't touch that box **yet**," Clara said with a calm that sounded too rehearsed.

The hallway smelled of wet wood and ***burned flowers***.

"If you hear three knocks, __do not answer__," I said.

Clara gripped the flashlight until her knuckles turned white.

"Then we'll have to cross the garden **_before dawn_**," she replied.

A letter appeared in the mailbox without a stamp, without a name, and without a shadow.

"*Nobody* is going to sleep tonight," I whispered.
